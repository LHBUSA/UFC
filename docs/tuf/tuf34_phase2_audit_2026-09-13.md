Classification: all 14 are `unverified`. The archive's exhibition rule needs the
bout's year to be fully recorded, and 2026 is not (future events). A narrower,
defensible basis exists — every 2026 event dated before the finale holds results,
so a professional bout between these fighters before 2026-09-13 would be in our
records — but it is a new rule and is proposed below, not applied.

## Root causes of the TUF 34 gaps

1. **Unfought finals recorded as source conflicts.** The draft ran before the finale;
   "no winner" was filed as a conflict instead of a future bout.
2. **Spelling splits break identity on the finalist.** "Mehemedeli" (bracket) vs
   "Mehemmedeli" (roster, ESPN); "Illimbek" (roster) vs "Ilimbek" (bracket, ESPN).
   The bantamweight final therefore has no id on one corner and cannot match its
   announced bout.
3. **Wikipedia is the only result source.** UFC.com published no TUF 34 recaps, ESPN
   carries no in-house events, and the broadcaster listing never states results.
4. **Classification rule depends on whole-year completeness**, which a current year
   cannot satisfy.
5. **Names printed differently from the records:** Tina Black (ring name of Valesca
   Machado), Gigi Canuto (records) vs Giovanna Canuto (draft).
6. **No times, no episodes** in the draft for any bout.
